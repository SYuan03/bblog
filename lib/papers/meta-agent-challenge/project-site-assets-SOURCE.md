# Meta-Agent Challenge project-site figures

The article uses two unmodified figures from the authors' official project site:

- `source-figure-2.jpg`: https://meta-agent-challenge.com/assets/dual-container.jpg (corresponds to paper Figure 2)
- `source-figure-4.png`: https://meta-agent-challenge.com/assets/pareto_frontier.png (official companion rendering of paper Figure 4)
- `source-figure-4-mobile.png`: the two complete panels of the preceding file, stacked vertically for mobile. Plot labels, points, and values are unchanged.

The official project's footer states “Released under the Apache License 2.0.” This was checked on 2026-10-19 at https://meta-agent-challenge.com/. The license text is included in `project-site-assets-LICENSE.txt`. The paper's arXiv v1 entry separately points to the nonexclusive distribution license; that PDF license is not the basis for copying these files. The article credits the official site and the corresponding paper figure beside each image.

The site's `eval_patterns.png` has different correlation values from arXiv v1 Figure 3. It is deliberately not used as a v1 paper figure.
